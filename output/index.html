About Me

I am a technical guy who understands artists and production. I have 10+ years of experience building pipelines on projects ranging from 3d series to high-end 2d features. Beyond tools and scripts, pipeline is a socio-technical system that reflects a company's culture.

I like to spend time understanding the organization's goals, strengths and weaknesses, and finding a path to success before writing a single line of code. Then I take an agile and pragmatic approach to development, striving to build simple and robust tools that make everyone's life easier without getting in the artist's way.

I work in English, French and Spanish.

These are some of the things I can help your studio with:

  • 2d / 3d / live-action hybrids
  • Stylized animation (shading, render and comp)
  • DCCs (Harmony, Nuke, Blender, Maya, Adobe Suite, etc.)
  • Production Tracking (SG / Flow, Kitsu, FTrack)
  • Software Development (Python, C++, JavaScript/HTML/CSS, SQL, etc.)
  • R&D (Maths, Computer Science, AI)
